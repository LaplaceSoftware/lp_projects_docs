# 010 — Glossary

Terms used consistently throughout this guide and in the portal itself.

---

## People and Organisations

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Customer** | A customer *company* that buys from SAMTIA — not an individual person |
| **Customer User** / Portal User | A person at a customer company who uses the Customer Portal |
| **Company Administrator** | A customer user who can also manage their own colleagues' accounts and tags |
| **Standard User** | A customer user who can shop and order but not manage colleagues |
| **Account Manager** | The SAMTIA employee who owns your company's account |

---

## Buying and Selling

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Draft Request** | A basket you are still building. Private, editable, not yet sent to SAMTIA |
| **Label** | The name you give a draft request or order |
| **RFQ** — Request for Quotation | A draft request formally submitted for pricing |
| **Quotation** | SAMTIA's priced response to an RFQ |
| **PO** — Purchase Order | Your formal order document, uploaded as a PDF to accept a quotation |
| **Order** | Confirmed business being fulfilled |
| **Target Price** | The price you say you are hoping to pay. Guidance for your Account Manager, not a commitment |
| **New Product Request** | A request for a product not in the catalogue |
| **Shared Request** | A draft basket you have flagged for your Account Manager's attention, without formally requesting a price |

---

## Sales Order Stages

| Stage | Plain meaning | Who acts next |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
| **Draft Request** | A draft request being built | You |
| **RFQ Submitted** | A price has been requested | Account Manager |
| **RFQ Revised** | You changed your request | Account Manager |
| **Quotation Issued** | A price has been offered | You |
| **PO Submitted** | You accepted and sent your order document | Account Manager |
| **Processing** | Confirmed and being fulfilled | Account Manager |
| **Delivered** | Complete | Nobody |
| **Rejected** | The request was refused | Nobody |
| **Cancelled** | The business was stopped | Nobody |

---

## Status Signals

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Schedule Status** | Timing signal from the requested delivery date: **Late**, **On Time** or **Upcoming** |
| **Requested Delivery Date** | When you need the goods |
| **Quotation Viewed Date** | When you opened the quotation |
| **Quotation Downloaded Date** | When you printed or downloaded the quotation |
| **Action Required** | A flag raised when you share a draft request or post a message |
| **Pending Resubmission** | You changed a quoted order but have not resubmitted it |
| **Return Reason** | Your Account Manager's explanation for sending an order back |

---

## Catalogue

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Product Category** | A branch of the browsing menu you navigate |
| **Brand** | A manufacturer you can browse by |
| **Product Variant** | A specific sellable version of a product — a size, a colour, a configuration |
| **Product Attribute** | A characteristic a product varies by, such as Size |
| **Product Attribute Value** | A specific option, such as Large |
| **Product Tag** | A label you can filter the catalogue by |
| **Product Badge** | A small marker on a product card, such as *New* or *Sale* |
| **Product Notice** | An important notice shown on a product page |
| **Featured Product** | A product promoted onto your Home page |
| **Requires Call** | A product needing a conversation before it can be ordered. Shown as *Call Required* |
| **Published** | Whether a product is visible to customers at all |

---

## People Management

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Account Enabled** / *Account Manager Approved* | The SAMTIA-controlled switch on your user account |
| **Portal Access Enabled** / *Status* | The switch your own Company Administrator controls |
| **User Tag** | A label your company uses to group its own people |
| **Invitation** | The e-mail sent to a new user with their access details and a QR code |
| **National ID / Iqama** | An alternative to an e-mail address for signing in |

---

## Communication

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Chat** | Live conversation with your Account Manager |
| **Comment** | A message attached permanently to a specific order |
| **Account Manager Note** | A standing statement about an order, written by SAMTIA and shown prominently to you |
| **Attachment** | A file attached to an order |
| **Notification** | An in-portal alert about something that happened |
| **Online / Away / Offline** | Whether your Account Manager is currently available |

---

## Screen Elements

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Request Selector** | The header control deciding which draft request receives products you add |
| **Action Items** | The list of things needing your attention, shown on Home |
| **Filters** | The panel narrowing a list by date ranges |
| **Banner** | A promotional image on your Home page, sometimes aimed at your company specifically |
