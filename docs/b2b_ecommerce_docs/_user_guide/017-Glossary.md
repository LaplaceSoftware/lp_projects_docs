# 017 — Glossary

Terms used consistently throughout this guide and in the platform itself.

---

## People and Organisations

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Client** | A customer *company* that buys from SAMTIA — not an individual person |
| **Client User** / Portal User | A person at a customer company who uses the Client Portal |
| **Company Administrator** | A customer user who can also manage their own colleagues' accounts and tags |
| **Standard User** | A customer user who can shop and order but not manage colleagues |
| **Account Manager** | The SAMTIA employee who owns a set of customer companies |
| **Pricing Manager** | A SAMTIA role focused on price lists and price rules |
| **Read Only Manager** | A SAMTIA role that can view but not change |
| **Super Administrator** | A SAMTIA role with full access including configuration |

---

## The Two Portals

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Client Portal** | The customer-facing website where companies browse and order |
| **Account Manager Portal (AMP)** | The internal website where SAMTIA staff serve customers |
| **Back-office** | SAMTIA's underlying business system, used by administrators. Not covered in this guide |

---

## Buying and Selling

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Wishlist** | A basket a customer is still building. Private, editable, not yet sent to SAMTIA |
| **Label** | The name a customer gives a wishlist or order |
| **RFQ** — Request for Quotation | A wishlist formally submitted for pricing |
| **Quotation** | SAMTIA's priced response to an RFQ |
| **PO** — Purchase Order | The customer's formal order document, uploaded as a PDF to accept a quotation |
| **Order** | Confirmed business being fulfilled |
| **Target Price** | The price a customer says they are hoping to pay. Guidance for the Account Manager, not a commitment |
| **Product Request** | A customer's request for a product not in the catalogue |
| **Shared wishlist** | A draft basket a customer has flagged for their Account Manager's attention, without formally requesting a price |

---

## Order Stages

| Stage | Plain meaning | Who acts next |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
| **Draft** | A wishlist being built | Customer |
| **RFQ Submitted** | A price has been requested | Account Manager |
| **RFQ Updated** | The customer changed their request | Account Manager |
| **Quotation Submitted** | A price has been offered | Customer |
| **PO Submitted** | The customer accepted and sent their order document | Account Manager |
| **In Progress** | Confirmed and being fulfilled | Account Manager |
| **Delivered** | Complete | Nobody |
| **Rejected** | The request was refused | Nobody |
| **Cancelled** | The business was stopped | Nobody |

---

## Status Signals

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Planning State** | Timing signal from the planned date: **Late**, **On Time** or **Upcoming** |
| **Planned Date** | When the customer needs the goods |
| **Reviewed Date** | When the customer opened the quotation |
| **Print Quotation Date** | When the customer printed or downloaded the quotation |
| **Needs Attention** | A flag raised when a customer shares a wishlist or posts a message. Cleared with **Reply Done** |
| **Pending Submit** | The customer changed a quoted order but has not resubmitted it |
| **Return Reason** | The Account Manager's explanation for sending an order back |

---

## Catalogue

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Product Category** | A branch of the browsing menu customers navigate |
| **Brand** | A manufacturer customers can browse by |
| **Variant** | A specific sellable version of a product — a size, a colour, a configuration |
| **Attribute** | A characteristic a product varies by, such as Size |
| **Attribute Value** | A specific option, such as Large |
| **Tag** *(product)* | A label customers can filter the catalogue by |
| **Ribbon / Badge** | A small marker on a product card, such as *New* or *Sale* |
| **Alert Message** | An important notice shown on a product page |
| **Featured Product** | A product promoted onto the customer Home page |
| **Requires Call** | A product needing a conversation before it can be ordered. Customers see *Call Required* |
| **Published** | Whether a product is visible to customers at all |

---

## Pricing

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Price List** | A set of prices offered to a group of customers |
| **Price Rule** | One entry on a price list, setting the price for a product or group |
| **Apply On** | What a price rule covers — a product, a variant, or a broader group |
| **Min Qty** | The minimum quantity before a price rule applies. Used for volume pricing |
| **Valid Period** | The dates between which a price rule applies |
| **Client Category** | A commercial segment of customers, carrying a default price list |

---

## People Management

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Active User** / *Account Manager Approved* | The SAMTIA-controlled switch on a customer user |
| **Portal Active** / *Status* | The customer-controlled switch on their own user |
| **User Tag** | A label a customer company uses to group its own people |
| **Invitation** | The e-mail sent to a new customer user with their access details and a QR code |
| **Identification Number** *(Iqama)* | An alternative to an e-mail address for signing in |

---

## Communication

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Chat** | Live conversation between a customer company and its Account Manager |
| **Comment** | A message attached permanently to a specific order |
| **Account Manager Comment** | A standing statement about an order, written by SAMTIA and shown prominently to the customer |
| **Attachment** | A file attached to an order or a client record |
| **Notification** | An in-portal alert about something that happened |
| **Online / Away / Offline** | Whether a person is currently using the portal |

---

## Screen Elements

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Wishlist selector** | The header control deciding which wishlist receives products you add |
| **Work Items** | The list of things needing your attention, shown on both home screens |
| **Column chooser** | The control for showing and hiding table columns |
| **Filters** | The panel narrowing a list by date ranges |
| **Banner** | A promotional image on a customer's Home page, optionally aimed at one customer and scheduled to run between dates |
